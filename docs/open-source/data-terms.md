# Government data terms (separate from MIT)

MIT covers this repository's code. It does **not** license USPTO/EUIPO data,
government logos, third-party works or personal information in registry records.

- [USPTO website/service Terms of Use](https://www.uspto.gov/terms-use-uspto-websites)
  apply unless superseded by a service-specific agreement. Use the official ODP
  bulk/API products, your own authorized credentials and current limits; website
  search interfaces are not bulk-download endpoints. Third-party authored material
  and images can have different rights. Check the product's current agreement and
  [ODP support](https://data.uspto.gov/support) before access/reuse/redistribution.
- [EUIPO API Conditions of Use, Annex I v2](https://euipo.europa.eu/tunnel-web/secure/webdav/guest/document_library/contentPdfs/law_and_practice/decisions_president/EX-25-12_Annex_1_v2_en.pdf)
  and [API getting started](https://dev.euipo.europa.eu/getting-started) govern
  approved API access. Follow your subscription's permissions, limits, notices and
  personal-data obligations. Check the current portal agreement when applying;
  do not assume private access grants unrestricted public redistribution.

References checked 2026-10-12. This guide is an access/reuse pointer, not a legal
clearance opinion; office terms and individual subscriptions can change.
